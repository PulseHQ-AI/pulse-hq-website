In plain words

How work actually gets done

No jargon, no lore — this is the whole path a piece of work takes, and what you receive or decide at each step.

Step 1

Brief

You say what you need in plain words — a topic, an offer, a run of posts. That's the whole job on your end.

Step 2

Research & verification

Sources are gathered first; then every claim is checked against them before anything is written, and comes back labelled: documented, mainstream interpretation, fringe claim, or not verified.

Step 3

Draft

You get finished posts with images, written in your business's voice — not outlines, prompts, or homework.

Step 4

Your approval

You see the real posts first. Nothing publishes without your say-so — approve it, or send it back for its rework round.

Step 5

Delivery package

Approved work arrives together as one tidy package, ready to publish — the posts, the images, and a short record of what was delivered.
