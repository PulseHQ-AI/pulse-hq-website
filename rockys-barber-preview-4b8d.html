Meet Rocky

The man behind the chair, in his own published story.

Rocky Sidloski has been cutting hair since 1982. He trained at the Moose Jaw Hair Design Academy and owned Studio 7 in Weyburn from 1992 to 2004.

He returned in 2013 to open Barbarians as a sole owner/operator shop — no staff, no hand-offs. When you book at Rocky's, Rocky is who cuts your hair.
