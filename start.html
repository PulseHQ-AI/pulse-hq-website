Getting started takes three steps

No calls to book and no pressure. Tell us about your business, show us what you've got, and pay the way that suits you — that's the whole front door.

Step 1

Tell us about your business

One short email tells us who you are and what you're after. Here's exactly what to put in it — no surprises:

Intake is by email — deliberately. No form, no ticket system: a person reads every first message, and a person writes back. Send one email with these six things and the intake is done:

  • 1Your name — who we're talking to.
  • 2Business name and town.
  • 3Your email and phone — however you'd rather hear back.
  • 4Facebook page or website, if you have one.
  • 5What you're interested in — the Founding Test Run, the Standard Pilot, a single service, or something else.
  • 6A few lines about it — what you need, in your own words.

Not sure about a line? Say so in the email — we'll sort it out in the reply.

Email us to start — getpulsehq@proton.me

Step 2

Show us your art

Already have a logo, artwork, photos, a menu, brand colours? Email them to us — we would much rather build with the real thing than invent a stand-in.

  • ✓Private, always. Your files are used for your work and nothing else.
  • ✓Never public without your say. Nothing you send us shows up anywhere unless you approve it first.
  • ✓Real beats stock. A real photo of your business will out-perform a stock image every time — if you've got them, we want them.

One ask: please only share files you have the right to share — your own photos and artwork, or ones you have permission to use.

Email your art — getpulsehq@proton.me
