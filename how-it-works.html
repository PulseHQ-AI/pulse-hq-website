Pulse HQ — Bring your online presence to life

How it works

Pulse HQ is a working office of specialists. You send us your business, the office produces finished work, and you approve everything before it goes anywhere. That's the whole idea — the rest of this page is just the details.

The specialists

Four kinds of specialist, one office

Every job passes through the desks it needs — no generalists guessing, no single tool doing everything.

Research

Gathers the facts first and checks every claim against its sources before anything is written — so what goes out in your name is solid.

Writing

Turns that research into posts written in your business's voice — the way you'd say it yourself, if you had the time.

Design

Makes the images that go with the words, matched to your brand, so every post looks like it belongs to you.

Front office

Looks after you: takes in your requests, packages up the finished work, and sends your reports. The people side of the office.

The flow

From your business to your page, in five steps

Step 1

You tell us about your business

What you do, who it's for, how you talk. That's the whole job on your end.

Step 2

The office gets to work

Research, writing, and design happen inside the office, in that order — facts first, then words, then images.

Step 3

One finished package arrives

Everything comes together as a single finished package with a simple download link — no loose files to hunt for.

Step 4

You approve it

Look it over and say yes, or send it back. Nothing publishes without your approval — ever.

Step 5

It goes out weekly

Approved work goes out on the weekly rhythm, and the next batch is already in the works.

Deliverables, not homework. What you receive is finished: posts with their images and hashtags, ready to post — or posted for you, per your plan. Never outlines, prompts, or half-done drafts for you to finish yourself.
