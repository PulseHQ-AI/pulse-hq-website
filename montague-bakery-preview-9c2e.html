About the Bakery

A local bakery and cafe in the heart of Montague.

Montague Bakery is an independent bakery and cafe at 520 Main St in Montague, Prince Edward Island, serving the community with retail and wholesale baking.

From sourdough loaves and cinnamon rolls to chicken pot pies and sweet trays for events, everything on the counter is freshly baked. Follow the bakery on Facebook for the latest specials and fresh-out-of-the-oven posts.
