Frequently Asked Questions

How do you cost less than an agency?

Structure. An agency’s price carries account managers, a sales team, and an office lease between you and the people doing the work. Pulse HQ has none of that layer — you pay for finished, researched work and one accountable approval step, not overhead. Don’t take our word for it: search social media management and compare the first fifteen results on Google with what’s included here.

How much does done-for-you social media cost?

At Pulse HQ: LiAIson starts with a $650 Pilot — 14 days, credited toward setup. After that, The Office is $950 a month, Office + Presence (the office plus 10 finished posts a month) is $1,450 a month, and Office + Everything (office, posts, and your website built and kept live) is $1,850 a month. Setup is $1,250, minus any credit you've already paid. Prices vary widely across the industry — our Research Department's check of published provider pricing found management averaging $100–$5,000 per month (WebFX), entry plans starting at $99 per month, and a $2,000–$6,000 per month range for small businesses at full-service agencies — so compare what's included, not just the number.

What exactly is included — what do you actually do?

Research, writing in your voice, images and design, hashtags, delivery as one finished package, ready for you to post, and reporting on what was delivered. Industry-wide, a retainer may also list strategy, community management, and strategy reviews — but no provider should be assumed to include everything. Your agreement lists exactly what's in yours, in writing, before work starts.

Do I have to create the content, supply photos, or film videos myself?

No. Creating the posts is the job you hand us — the crew researches, writes, and designs them. If you have real photos of your business, share them at intake and we'll build around them; if you don't, the crew creates the images to match. No filming is required for our Facebook post packages.

Which platforms will you make content for — and which should I be on?

Our Office + Presence and Office + Everything tiers cover Facebook and Instagram — every post is written once and adapted for both. Ask us about other platforms. As general guidance from the providers our research checked: local and service businesses usually do best on Facebook plus a Google Business Profile, visual brands on Instagram, business-to-business on LinkedIn, and products aimed at younger audiences on TikTok. That's guidance, not a guarantee — the right mix depends on where your customers actually are.

How many posts will I get — and how often are they delivered?

The LiAIson Pilot is the office itself — 14 days of leads followed up and promises tracked, no posts bundled in. On Office + Presence and Office + Everything: 10 finished posts a month, delivered in weekly batches for your approval — you post them; we never publish for clients. The Office tier carries no posts at all; it's the working office on its own. Across the industry plans our research checked, 10 to 30 posts per month is the documented range.

Do I approve posts first — how much of my time will it take, and do I share passwords?

Yes — you approve everything before it's delivered, and nothing is delivered without that approval. Your time is two touchpoints: drop the brief, then make one approval call per piece. And you never share passwords with us — you don't need to: we hand you finished work, and posting it happens from your own account, by you.

How long before I see results — and how will you report it?

You'll see reporting from the start: the Pilot ends with a short report on what was delivered, turnaround, and approval results, and the monthly tiers include a weekly report on what shipped, what's queued, and what's next. We don't promise a results timeline. Industry guidance points to a 60–90 day window for early engagement to move — that's provider guidance, not a measured guarantee — so agree the metrics that matter to you before signing.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

Our standard packages cover creating your posts — the writing, the images, the look — and delivering them finished for you to post. Posting them, and replies to comments and messages, stay with you. That's common across the industry: some providers include reply coverage, others charge for it separately, and some leave it with the client entirely. If you need it covered, ask before signing so it's written into your agreement.

Is ad spend included — do I need ads as well?

No. Our prices cover organic content — the posts themselves. Ad spend is a separate cost, paid directly to the platform if you choose to run ads, and ad management isn't part of the standard packages. That's the industry norm too: management, ad management, and ad spend are three separate lines unless a quote states otherwise.

How long is the contract — can I cancel, and who owns my accounts and content if I leave?

The monthly tiers are month-to-month — cancel with 30 days' notice, no lock-in. You own the content delivered to you and you keep your own accounts: Pulse HQ never needs access to your accounts and never asks for your passwords. If you leave, your pages, posts, and history stay yours.

What if I don't like a post?

Tell us, and it gets reworked. On the Pilot, if the calibration batch doesn't sound like you, the pilot fee comes back — that's the guarantee it runs on.

How fast is it?

The LiAIson Pilot runs 14 days. On the monthly tiers, finished packages arrive on a weekly rhythm.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe’s secure checkout — the payment links are on the Start page — or by PayPal or e-transfer. The Pilot and Setup are paid before work begins; the monthly tiers are billed monthly.

Is my information private?

Yes. What you send us is used to do your work and nothing else. See the Privacy page for the plain details.
