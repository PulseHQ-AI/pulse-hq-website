Frequently Asked Questions

How much does done-for-you social media cost?

At Pulse HQ: the Founding Test Run is $199 for 28 posts over 14 days, the standard Pilot is $650 flat, setup is $1,250 minus any credit you've already paid, and the Core retainer is $1,450 per month. Prices vary widely across the industry — our Research Department's check of published provider pricing found management averaging $100–$5,000 per month (WebFX), entry plans starting at $99 per month, and a $2,000–$6,000 per month range for small businesses at full-service agencies — so compare what's included, not just the number.

What exactly is included — what do you actually do?

Research, writing in your voice, images and design, hashtags, scheduling of the posts you approve, delivery as one finished package, and reporting on what shipped. Industry-wide, a retainer may also list strategy, community management, and strategy reviews — but no provider should be assumed to include everything. Your agreement lists exactly what's in yours, in writing, before work starts.

Do I have to create the content, supply photos, or film videos myself?

No. Creating the posts is the job you hand us — the crew researches, writes, and designs them. If you have real photos of your business, share them at intake and we'll build around them; if you don't, the crew creates the images to match. No filming is required for our Facebook post packages.

Which platforms will you post to — and which should I be on?

Our packages are built for Facebook pages — ask us about other platforms. As general guidance from the providers our research checked: local and service businesses usually do best on Facebook plus a Google Business Profile, visual brands on Instagram, business-to-business on LinkedIn, and products aimed at younger audiences on TikTok. That's guidance, not a guarantee — the right mix depends on where your customers actually are.

How many posts will I get — and how often will you post?

On the Founding Test Run: 28 finished posts over 14 days. On the Pilot: a voice-calibration batch first, then two weeks of deliverables. On the Core retainer: a weekly batch, sized in your agreement. Across the industry plans our research checked, 10 to 30 posts per month is the documented range — confirm the exact count for the package you buy.

Do I approve posts first — how much of my time will it take, and do I share passwords?

Yes — you approve everything before it goes out, and nothing publishes without that approval. Your time is two touchpoints: drop the brief, then make one approval call per piece. And never share passwords: access is delegated through official tools like Meta Business Suite partner access. The providers our research checked work the same way — approval first, partner access instead of passwords.

How long before I see results — and how will you report it?

You'll see reporting from the start: the Test Run ends with a short report on what was delivered, turnaround, and approval results, and the Core retainer includes a weekly report on what shipped, what's queued, and what's next. We don't promise a results timeline. Industry guidance points to a 60–90 day window for early engagement to move — that's provider guidance, not a measured guarantee — so agree the metrics that matter to you before signing.

Will you reply to comments and messages?

Our standard packages cover creating, scheduling, and publishing your approved posts — replies to comments and messages stay with you unless your written agreement says otherwise. That's common across the industry: some providers include reply coverage, others charge for it separately, and some leave it with the client entirely. If you need it covered, ask before signing so it's written into your agreement.

Is ad spend included — do I need ads as well?

No. Our prices cover organic content — the posts themselves. Ad spend is a separate cost, paid directly to the platform if you choose to run ads, and ad management isn't part of the standard packages. That's the industry norm too: management, ad management, and ad spend are three separate lines unless a quote states otherwise.

How long is the contract — can I cancel, and who owns my accounts and content if I leave?

The Core retainer is month-to-month — cancel with 30 days' notice, no lock-in. You own the content delivered to you and you keep your own accounts: Pulse HQ works through delegated access, never ownership, and never asks for your passwords. If you leave, your pages, posts, and history stay yours.

What exactly do I get?

Finished work, not homework. Every package arrives complete: posts researched, written, and designed for your business, each with its image and hashtags, delivered as one tidy package with a simple download link. Website and Facebook page setup work is delivered the same way — built, finished, and handed over.
