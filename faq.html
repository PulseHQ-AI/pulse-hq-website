Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly do I get?

Finished work, not homework. Every package arrives complete: posts researched, written, and designed for your business, each with its image and hashtags, delivered as one tidy package with a simple download link. Website and Facebook page setup work is delivered the same way — built, finished, and handed over.

How many posts do I get?

On the Founding Test Run: 28 finished Facebook posts with images over 14 days. On the standard Pilot: a calibration batch first, so we lock onto your voice before anything else is written. On the Core retainer: a weekly batch, sized in your agreement.

Which platforms do you cover?

Our packages are built for Facebook pages. Ask us about other platforms.

Do I approve everything before it goes out?

Yes — everything. Nothing is ever published without your approval. If we don't hear back inside the approval window, the work simply waits. It never goes out on its own.

What if I don't like a post?

Tell us, and it gets reworked. The Founding Test Run includes one rework round per post. On the Pilot, if the calibration batch doesn't sound like you, the pilot fee comes back — that's the guarantee it runs on.

What does it cost?

Founding Test Run: $199 for 28 posts over 14 days (limited to 2 businesses). The $199 is credited toward setup if you continue within 7 days. Standard Pilot: $650 flat, credited toward setup. Setup: $1,250, minus any credit you've already paid. Core retainer: $1,450 per month, month-to-month. Custom arrangements are quoted in writing before any work starts.

Who owns the content?

You do. The posts, images, and pages delivered to you are yours. Our office, tools, and internal processes remain ours — you receive the finished work, not homework and not software to run.

How fast is it?

The Founding Test Run runs 14 days. Under the Core retainer, finished packages arrive on a weekly rhythm.

How do I pay?

By e-transfer or PayPal. The Test Run, Pilot, and Setup are paid before work begins; the Core retainer is billed monthly.

Can I cancel?

Yes. The Core retainer is month-to-month — cancel with 30 days' notice, no lock-in.

Is my information private?

Yes. What you send us is used to do your work and nothing else. See the Privacy page for the plain details.

How do I reach you?

Email getpulsehq@proton.me. We reply within one business day.
