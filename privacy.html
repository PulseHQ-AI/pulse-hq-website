Privacy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

What we collect. Only what the work needs: your name and contact details, information about your business, and any photos, logos, or files you send us.

Why. To do your work, deliver it to you, and reply to you. Nothing else.

What we never do. We never sell your information. We never use your business's materials for another client.

Sharing. Your deliverables are sent to you through a private download link addressed to you. We use ordinary business services (email, file storage) to operate; your information passes through those only as needed to work with you.

How long we keep it. While you're a client, and afterwards only as reasonable business records. Ask us to delete your information and we will, except records the law requires us to keep.

Your rights. Email us and you can see what we hold about you, correct it, or ask for it to be deleted.
