Standing matters

Feud — Council hears the western dock question on Tuesday. Arthur wants it kept open; Mara wants it closed safe; June Mercer wants the workshop.
Hope — Thursday at the Gull & Lantern: planning a first music night in the upstairs room. The piano's condition is the town's smallest standing wager.
Debt — Ruth Calder's Harbour Hall archive restoration still needs its funding sorted. Volunteers are being sought to catalogue the boxes.
